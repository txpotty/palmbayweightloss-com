    How Hair Growth Treatment Works in Merritt Island, FL

    Hair Loss Treatment for Men in Merritt Island

    Most male hair loss is androgenetic alopecia. DHT, made from testosterone, gradually miniaturizes follicles in genetically sensitive men, starting at the temples or crown. Finasteride lowers DHT, and minoxidil supports growth directly. Combined use tends to work better than one alone.

    Hair Loss Treatment for Women

    In women, thinning usually shows up as a wider part or less volume on top, while the front hairline stays put. Pattern loss is the usual reason, though low ferritin, thyroid trouble and postpartum shedding can mimic it. Minoxidil is the main FDA-approved option for women. Some women add off-label spironolactone or low-dose oral minoxidil.

    Finasteride + Minoxidil in Merritt Island

    Finasteride is an FDA-approved 1 mg daily tablet for male pattern hair loss, and it lowers scalp and blood DHT by roughly two-thirds. Topical minoxidil extends the growth phase and thickens individual hairs. The pairing is the standard. A compounded combination spray is another route, though it is not FDA-approved.

    Dutasteride + Minoxidil

    Dutasteride blocks both forms of the enzyme that makes DHT, so it lowers DHT by more than 90 percent in the blood. It is FDA-approved for an enlarged prostate and used off-label for hair loss, most often in men who did not get enough from finasteride after a year. It lingers. Its half-life is about five weeks, so screening and side effect planning take more care. Minoxidil is added for growth.

    Minoxidil + GHK-Cu in Merritt Island

    Some people want a topical-only plan with no DHT blocker. In this blend, minoxidil drives regrowth and GHK-Cu, a copper peptide, has early research suggesting it may help the scalp environment. It is compounded. It is not FDA-approved. It suits women, and men avoiding finasteride.

    How a Provider Chooses Between Treatments

    Your provider weighs sex, age, pattern, speed of loss and medical history. They also ask about pregnancy plans, worries about sexual side effects, past depression and whether you would rather take a pill or apply something to your scalp twice a day. Most men begin with the finasteride and minoxidil pairing. Minoxidil is the usual first step for women.

    What to Expect Over Time in Merritt Island

    Hair grows about half an inch a month. Visible change takes three to six months, and full results take about a year, so patience is part of the plan. Some people shed more in weeks two through eight of minoxidil, which usually means follicles are cycling into a new growth phase. Stopping treatment lets the loss resume.
